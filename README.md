# User Guide for Testers and Contributors

This document provides comprehensive instructions for individuals intending to access the application and participate in the donation process. Please follow the guidelines below to proceed.

### Phase 1: Digital Wallet Initialization (MetaMask)
This application operates on Web3 technology, which necessitates the use of an Ethereum digital wallet.
1. Open a compatible web browser (Google Chrome or Brave is highly recommended).
2. Download and install the **MetaMask** browser extension from the official website: [metamask.io](https://metamask.io).
3. Launch MetaMask and proceed to create a new wallet. Ensure that you securely record and store your 12-word Seed Phrase. If you already possess an active wallet, you may bypass this step.
4. Click on the Network selection icon located in the upper left corner of the MetaMask interface, and enable the **"Show test networks"** option.
5. Switch your current network configuration from the Ethereum Mainnet to the **Sepolia** network.

### Phase 2: Acquiring Testnet Funds (Sepolia ETH)
All blockchain interactions, including creating campaigns or initiating donations, require a balance to cover transaction costs, commonly referred to as Gas Fees. As this application is deployed on a testing environment (Sepolia), you can obtain simulated funds free of charge.
1. Visit a designated Faucet website to request testnet funds. Recommended platforms include:
   - [Google Web3 Sepolia Faucet](https://cloud.google.com/application/web3/faucet/ethereum/sepolia)
   - [Alchemy Sepolia Faucet](https://sepoliafaucet.com/) (Registration for a free account is required)
2. Open your MetaMask extension and click on your account name (e.g., Account 1) to copy your wallet address, which typically begins with `0x`.
3. Paste the copied address into the provided field on the Faucet website and submit your request.
4. Please wait for a few moments until the Sepolia ETH balance is credited to your MetaMask wallet.

### Phase 3: Executing a Donation
1. Navigate to the provided URL of this decentralized donation application.
2. Locate and click the **"Connect Wallet"** button situated in the top right corner of the interface.
3. Upon the appearance of the MetaMask authorization prompt, click **Next**, followed by **Connect**.
4. Browse the homepage and select an active fundraising campaign that you wish to support.
5. Enter your intended donation amount (for instance, `0.001` ETH).
6. Should you prefer your identity to remain undisclosed on the public interface, check the *Anonymous* option before clicking the **Donate** button.
7. A subsequent MetaMask prompt will appear requesting transaction approval. Review the estimated costs and click **Confirm**.
8. Allow approximately 10 to 15 seconds for the loading process to conclude. This duration is necessary for the blockchain network to permanently record your transaction.
9. The transaction is now complete. The application interface will automatically refresh to reflect your contribution within the system.
